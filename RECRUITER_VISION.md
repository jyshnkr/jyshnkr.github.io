## Complete Flow: What Recruiters Experience

### 0-3 Seconds: Hero
- **See:** "I BUILD SOFTWARE THAT SOLVES REAL PROBLEMS AT SCALE"
- **Think:** "Okay, this person has a clear value prop"
- **Action:** Keep scrolling

### 3-10 Seconds: Quick Scan Bar Appears
- **See:** "5+ years | Amazon • AWS | Austin, TX | Available"
- **Think:** "Good experience, good companies, available"
- **Action:** Scroll to see more

### 10-20 Seconds: Work Sections
- **See:** Problem → Solution → Impact format
- **Think:** "This person thinks analytically, shows results"
- **Action:** Scroll to impact

### 20-30 Seconds: Impact Section
- **See:** Context-rich achievements with problems solved
- **Think:** "Impressive numbers with clear context"
- **Action:** Scroll to contact

### 30-40 Seconds: Contact Section
- **See:** Email with copy button, prominent QR code
- **Think:** "Easy to contact, QR code is smart"
- **Action:** Copy email or scan QR code

### 40-45 Seconds: "One More Thing"
- **See:** Personal philosophy
- **Think:** "This person has passion and purpose. I'll remember them."
- **Action:** Bookmark or save contact

## Key Improvements Summary

| Current | Proposed | Impact |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Generic hero | Value proposition first | 3-second clarity |
| Just numbers | Problem-solution-impact | Shows thinking |
| Basic contact | Copy button + QR prominence | Frictionless |
| No closer | "Why I Build" section | Memorable |
| No mobile shortcuts | "Skip to Contact" button | Mobile-friendly |
| Scattered info | Quick scan bar | 10-second summary |

---

## Design Principles Applied

1. **Clarity First** - Value prop is the FIRST thing they see
2. **Context Matters** - Numbers need stories
3. **Frictionless Actions** - One-click copy, prominent QR
4. **Memorable Closer** - "Why I Build" makes you unforgettable
5. **Mobile-First** - Quick actions for mobile recruiters
6. **Show Thinking** - Problem-solution format shows HOW you work

---

## The Steve Jobs Test

✅ **3-Second Test:** Can they understand your value? YES - Value prop first
✅ **10-Second Test:** Can they get key info? YES - Quick scan bar
✅ **Action Test:** Can they contact you easily? YES - Copy button + QR
✅ **Memory Test:** Will they remember you? YES - "Why I Build" section
